# Cover — 咪咪上厕所

Children's picture book cover illustration, soft watercolor style, warm and bright colors, round shapes, simple background, suitable for 0-5 years old kids, consistent character design, similar to Axel Scheffler's "Pip and Posy" style.

Title: "Mimi Goes to the Toilet" (咪咪上厕所)

A round chubby orange-yellow tabby kitten (MIMI) with cream-white stripes, fluffy big tail, pink-inside pointy ears, big amber eyes, tiny pink bean nose, and thin whiskers, wearing sky-blue overalls with a star on the chest pocket and yellow buttons on straps. She is sitting proudly on a cute little yellow duck-shaped potty, her feet dangling and swinging happily, a big proud smile on her face, tail raised and wagging. Next to her is a roll of toilet paper and a small picture book on the floor. The background is a warm, bright bathroom with cute little fish tiles on the wall, a small step stool near the sink, and cheerful sunshine coming through a small window. The title text "咪咪上厕所" is displayed at the top in playful rounded Chinese font.
