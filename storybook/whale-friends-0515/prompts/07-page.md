# Page 7 — 壳壳爷爷的启发

Children's picture book illustration, soft watercolor style, warm and bright colors, round shapes, simple background, suitable for 0-5 years old kids, consistent character design, similar to Axel Scheffler's "Pip and Posy" style.

An old large dark green sea turtle (GRANDPA KEKE) with patterned shell, reading glasses, and old leather pouch sits side by side with a round chubby light blue whale (BOBO) with creamy white belly and orange-red scarf on a large flat rock covered in soft moss. GRANDPA KEKE has opened his leather pouch, revealing many beautiful colorful seashells inside. He holds up two shells with his flipper: one is an open, lively-looking fan scallop shell, and the other is a quiet, spiral-shaped conch shell. BOBO looks at the two shells intently, his big deep blue eyes gradually showing understanding and realization, the light of comprehension dawning in them. Soft underwater light illuminates the scene.
