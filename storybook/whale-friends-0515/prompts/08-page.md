# Page 8 — 波波的改变

Children's picture book illustration, soft watercolor style, warm and gentle colors, round shapes, simple background, suitable for 0-5 years old kids, consistent character design, similar to Axel Scheffler's "Pip and Posy" style.

At the coral cave entrance. A round chubby light blue whale (BOBO) with creamy white belly and orange-red scarf floats quietly and still near the cave opening, his expression gentle and sincere, very different from his earlier wild energy. He carefully blows a delicate string of tiny shimmering bubbles from his mouth, the bubbles drift slowly and gently into the dark cave, each little bubble reflecting rainbow-colored light. Deep inside the cave, a small semi-transparent pinkish-purple jellyfish (PAOPAO) has one curious little eye peeking out from the darkness, the tip of one tentacle glowing with a tiny flash of light, showing the first sign of curiosity and willingness to come out. The scene is quiet and hopeful.
