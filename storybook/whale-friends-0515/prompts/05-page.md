# Page 5 — 情绪低谷

Children's picture book illustration, soft watercolor style, slightly muted but still warm colors, round shapes, simple background, suitable for 0-5 years old kids, consistent character design, similar to Axel Scheffler's "Pip and Posy" style.

A beautiful but quiet coral cave entrance. Deep inside the coral cave, a small semi-transparent pinkish-purple jellyfish (PAOPAO) is curled up with her glow dimmed to almost nothing, tentacles tightly coiled together, looking sad and withdrawn. Outside the cave entrance, a round chubby light blue whale (BOBO) with creamy white belly and orange-red scarf lies flat on the coral ledge, his round flippers resting on the coral, his big deep blue eyes filled with guilt and sadness, his blowhole completely still with no water spout, his scarf drooping limply. A few small anemone flowers bloom quietly around the cave entrance. The lighting is softer, more subdued, conveying a quiet melancholy.
