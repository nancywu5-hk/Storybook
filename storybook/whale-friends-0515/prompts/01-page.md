# Page 1 — 日常场景建立

Children's picture book illustration, soft watercolor style, warm and bright colors, round shapes, simple background, suitable for 0-5 years old kids, consistent character design, similar to Axel Scheffler's "Pip and Posy" style.

A wide, bright underwater world with light blue ocean water and golden sunlight dappling down from the surface above. A round chubby light blue whale (BOBO) with a creamy white belly and an orange-red scarf with golden starfish embroidery swims happily through the water, flippers spread wide, a cheerful little water spout shooting from his blowhole. Surrounding him are colorful coral reefs in pink, orange, and yellow, cute little starfish on rocks, scattered seashells on the sandy bottom, and a few small tropical fish swimming in the distance. The atmosphere is cheerful, bright, and full of life.
