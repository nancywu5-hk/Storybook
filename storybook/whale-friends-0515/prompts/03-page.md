# Page 3 — 波波太热情

Children's picture book illustration, soft watercolor style, warm and bright colors, round shapes, simple background, suitable for 0-5 years old kids, consistent character design, similar to Axel Scheffler's "Pip and Posy" style.

Underwater scene with swirling water currents. A round chubby light blue whale (BOBO) with creamy white belly and orange-red scarf is excitedly swimming in fast circles around a small jellyfish, his big tail swishing powerfully creating visible water swirls and currents. His mouth is open wide chattering non-stop, his blowhole shooting an extra tall excited water spout. A semi-transparent pinkish-purple jellyfish (PAOPAO) with coral-pink flower hair clip is being tossed around by the water currents, her delicate tentacles tangled and swirled, her bell cap squeezed tight in fear, looking overwhelmed and scared by all the commotion. Small fish in the background are also being swept around by the swirling water.
