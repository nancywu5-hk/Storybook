# Page 10 — 温暖结尾

Children's picture book illustration, soft watercolor style, warm golden dreamy colors, round shapes, simple background, suitable for 0-5 years old kids, consistent character design, similar to Axel Scheffler's "Pip and Posy" style.

A peaceful, beautiful evening underwater scene. The deep blue water is filled with golden light spots filtering down from the water surface like twinkling stars. A round chubby light blue whale (BOBO) with creamy white belly and orange-red scarf with golden starfish floats peacefully and contentedly above a field of beautiful colorful sea anemone flowers in pink, purple, and white. His expression is calm and satisfied with a gentle smile, tiny little bubbles floating up softly from his blowhole. A semi-transparent pinkish-purple jellyfish (PAOPAO) with coral-pink flower hair clip floats right beside him, her whole body glowing with warm soft pink light, one tentacle naturally and gently resting on BOBO's round flipper. In the far background on a distant coral reef, an old dark green sea turtle (GRANDPA KEKE) with reading glasses watches them from afar with a warm knowing smile. The entire scene radiates peace, warmth, love, and the beauty of gentle friendship.
