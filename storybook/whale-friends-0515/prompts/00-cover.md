# Cover — 鲸鱼的伙伴们

Children's picture book cover illustration, soft watercolor style, warm and bright colors, round shapes, simple background, suitable for 0-5 years old kids, consistent character design, similar to Axel Scheffler's "Pip and Posy" style.

Title: "Whale's Friends" (鲸鱼的伙伴们)

A round chubby light blue whale (BOBO) with a creamy white belly and an orange-red scarf with a golden starfish embroidered on the end, swimming joyfully in crystal clear turquoise ocean water, mouth wide open in a big smile, a cute little water spout shooting from his blowhole. Next to him, a semi-transparent pinkish-purple jellyfish (PAOPAO) with a ruffled bell cap and coral-pink flower hair clip, floating gracefully with tentacle tips glowing softly. Colorful tropical fish, bright coral, orange starfish, and pastel sea flowers surround them. Warm golden sunlight rays filter down from the water surface above. The title text "鲸鱼的伙伴们" is displayed at the top in playful rounded Chinese font.
