Warm sunset light filtering through the water surface. An old large dark green sea turtle (GRANDPA KEKE) with a thick shell covered in patterns like an ancient map slowly swims in from the right side of the scene. He wears reading glasses on the tip of his nose, and has a small old leather pouch tied to his shell. His expression is warm and gentle with deep smile wrinkle lines on his face.
