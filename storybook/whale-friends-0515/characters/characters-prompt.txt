1. BOBO (Little Blue Whale, protagonist): Round and chubby light blue whale with a creamy white belly, smooth skin with a soft sheen, big round deep blue eyes with white highlights, a small blowhole on top of the head, a gently curved smiling mouth, two short round flippers on each side, a wide flat tail like a little fan.
